Da Vinci rules (jo software check karta hai)

  1. Liquidity block banta hai. Price kisi purane high/low ki liquidity leta hai (sweep), phir foran displacement deta hai: previous candle ke low/high ke paar close, ya imbalance (FVG). Wo high/low LB hai.
  2. LB ke neeche buildup. Price pichla low tod deti hai (BOS, early sellers induce hote hain), phir LB se neeche lower high banati hai. Bullish mein ye ulta hota hai.
  3. Lows par liquidity engineer hoti hai. Neeche wala low respect hota hai (baad wala low us se upar rehta hai), to uske neeche liquidity rehti hai. Yahi target hai.
  4. Buildup liya, entry. Price buildup highs le kar LB mein aati hai: wahan sell. Stop LB ke upar, target lows. RR kam az kam 1:3 hona chahiye.

Setup cancel ho jata hai agar price LB ke upar close kare, entry se pehle lows le le, ya bohot der lage. Yeh course ki ek mechanical approximation hai. Discretionary cheezein (HTF narrative, "feel") isme poori nahi aatin.

Educational tool. Financial advice nahi. Backtest future profit ki guarantee nahi deta. Data: Yahoo Finance futures (GC=F, NQ=F, YM=F), jo spot/CFD prices se thoda alag ho sakta hai.
