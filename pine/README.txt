DA VINCI INDICATOR (TRADINGVIEW)
================================

TradingView par lagane ka tareeqa
---------------------------------
1. TradingView kholo, koi chart (misaal: OANDA:XAUUSD, 5m) kholo.
2. Neeche "Pine Editor" tab kholo.
3. Editor mein jo likha ho sab mita do, phir "Da Vinci indicator.pine" file ka poora text copy karke paste karo.
4. "Save" dabao (naam: Da Vinci), phir "Add to chart".

Chart par kya dikhega
---------------------
- Neela box: Liquidity Block (LB)
- Lal box: stop loss zone, hara box: target zone, label par SELL/BUY aur RR
- Upar daayein table: is chart ki history par trades, win rate aur avg R

Alert (phone par TradingView app se)
------------------------------------
1. Chart par "Alert" (ghadi icon) dabao.
2. Condition: "Da Vinci" -> "Any alert() function call".
3. Notifications mein "Notify on app" tick karo. Create.
Jab bhi setup arm hoga, phone par entry/SL/TP ke saath alert aayega.
(Free TradingView plan mein alerts ki tadaad limited hoti hai.)

Settings
--------
- "Session filter": case studies New York session (subah 8-11 New York time) par focus karti hain.
  Session hamesha New York time mein hai (kisi bhi broker ke chart par), Pakistan time mein taqreeban
  shaam 6-9 baje (sardiyon mein), garmiyon mein 5-8 baje.
- "Entry", "Stop buffer", "Target", "Minimum RR": PC app jaisi settings.

Ye educational tool hai, financial advice nahi. Table mein negative R ho to us chart par is par trade mat karo.
